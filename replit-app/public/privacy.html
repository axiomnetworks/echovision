Data handling and limits

What is sent

Only after you start and consent, short clips and your target/change request pass through our Replit backend to Meta's SAM 3.1 and Standard Muse Spark. Audio is removed. A tokenizer-only check also processes the reasoning input. No model key is sent to your browser.

Meta's billing notice says content submitted using free credits may be used for product improvement. Standard is the selected model variant, but that does not remove this free-credit condition. Do not upload sensitive, confidential, personal or third-party material without the rights and permission required. The restricted demo allows only nonpersonal samples unless the operator separately enables personal camera use.

What stays

Our backend keeps raw clips only in memory and temporary files during processing, then removes those files. We do not build a raw-video library. Session state and recent derived text are temporary in server memory. This tab's text history clears when the tab closes or you clear it. A private database keeps an allowance identifier and used-call counter, not your video, target, description or history. Replit infrastructure may retain operational logs. Meta processing and retention follow its own terms; this app is not a zero-retention service.

When it stops

Stop, a hidden page, camera failure, the ten-minute session limit or the approved clip allowance ends capture. A request already sent to Meta may still complete and incur usage. No automatic model retries. The service may shut down when its approved time or budget ends. Restarting a browser does not reset the shared allowance.

What it cannot promise

Clips are delayed samples, not every-frame live understanding. The camera pauses while analysis is pending, so events can happen in a gap. Occlusion, a moving camera, lighting, clip boundaries and model mistakes can cause missed or false detections. An object disappearing never proves a person left a room. A possible event is not confirmed until supported across two clips, which is still not a guarantee. Use no safety-critical, navigation, medical, face-recognition or identity decisions.
